(`assets-src/audio/ambience/`) and 30 CC0 instrument samples, VCSL / VSCO 2 CE (`assets-src/audio/samples/`).

## Myths, legends and characters
Content rules: bible §3.4 (original or public-domain only; no deities of living religions as fighters; no modern
adaptations). Categories used by the 27 champions (`src/content/champions.ts`, names from
`src/content/generated/bible.json`):
- **Public-domain myths and legends:** Greek myth (Olympus, and the Fates who frame the story), Japanese folklore
  (Yomi), Norse myth (Ragnarök), ancient Egyptian myth and the Arabian Nights (Sunken Sands), East Asian folk legend
  (the demon-queller in Inferno), and **Vietnamese legends** (Lạc Việt, four champions), treated as heroic and
  respectful per bible §3.4.
- **Original characters in genre realms:** wuxia (Jianghu), xianxia / tu tiên (Cloud Peaks), high fantasy
  (Dragonmarch), war chronicle with archetypes, not real persons (Chronicle Front), cyberpunk (Neon Tomorrow), space
  opera (Starfall), and an original demon in Inferno.
- The Vietnamese localisation avoided phrases that echo modern wuxia works (DECISIONS #20).
